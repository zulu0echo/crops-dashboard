 This guide outlines the fees, transaction limits, and spending rules associated with your Gnosis Pay Card. It covers purchases, withdrawals, FX rates, card replacements, and the difference between card fees and blockchain gas fees.  To make your experience predictable and fair, Gnosis Pay keeps its fees transparent and its limits clearly defined. This article breaks down everything you need to know about card usage - from spending and withdrawals to replacements and FX charges.  Daily Purchase Fees (In-Store & Online) No transaction fees when spending with your card 1:1 stablecoin usage — a €10 purchase = 10 EURe (or equivalent) Applies to both online and in-person purchases    Foreign Exchange (FX) Fees No added fees from Gnosis Pay for currency conversions Visa’s exchange rate is applied automatically at the time of purchase Pro Tip:   When travelling, always select the local currency at payment terminals for better rates. Daily limit runs on a rolling 24 hour period and does not reset at 00:00   Limit Type Maximum Daily Spending Limit 8,000 EURe/GBPe/USDCe Single Transaction Limit 5,000 EURe/GBPe/USDCe Contactless "Tap-to-Pay" country dependent (physical card only) Mobile Wallet Note: The contactless limit applies only to physical cards and can vary.
