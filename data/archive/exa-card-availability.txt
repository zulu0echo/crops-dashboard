All Collections Exa Card Card availability and usage Card availability and usage Written by Mate Updated over 2 weeks ago Table of contents To get an Exa Card, you must go to the Card section and 
request your virtual card. First, you will be asked to go through the validation process, but don't worry—it won't take more than 3 minutes. After that, you'll be able to issue your card. Please keep 
in mind that you will need to have funds in your account to enable your Exa Card. ​ Eligible Countries for Card Issuance The Exa Card can be issued to residents of the following countries: Antigua & 
Barbuda, Argentina, Australia, Bahamas, Barbados, Belize, Bolivia, Brazil, Cayman Islands, Colombia, Costa Rica, Côte d'Ivoire, Dominican Republic, Ecuador, El Salvador, Ghana, Grenada, Guatemala, 
Guyana, Honduras, Hong Kong, Japan, Kenya, Malaysia, Mexico, Morocco, New Zealand, Nigeria (requires conditional approval), Pakistan, Panama, Paraguay, Peru, Philippines, Saint Kitts and Nevis, Saint 
Lucia, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, Senegal, Singapore, South Africa, Suriname, Trinidad and Tobago, Turks & Caicos Islands, UAE, Uganda, Uruguay, Zambia. Eligible US States for Card Issuance 
The Exa Card can be issued to residents of the following US states: Alabama, Alaska, Arkansas, California, Colorado, Connecticut, Florida, Hawaii, Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Kentucky, Maine, 
Massachusetts, Michigan, Minnesota, Montana, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, New York, North Carolina, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Carolina, Texas, Utah, Virginia, West Virginia, Wyoming. 
Ineligible Countries for Card Issuance The Exa Card cannot be issued to residents of the following countries: Bangladesh, Belarus, Cuba, India, Iran, Iraq, Israel, Mainland China, Myanmar, Nepal, 
Nicaragua, North Korea, Russia, Syria, Turkey, Ukraine, Venezuela, Vietnam. ​ Where Can You Spend Using Your Exa Card? You can use your Exa Card anywhere in the world, except in the following 
countries: Cuba Iran North Korea Russia Syria Ukraine Venezuela Your Exa Card works anywhere Visa is accepted, covering over 150 million merchants across more than 150 countries. Did this answer your
