Oobit is available in 75 countries and territories across six continents, and we add new markets regularly. The table shows what you can do in each country. The app always shows what's available to you.  In all 75 countries you can get the Oobit Visa card, make bank transfers, send to Oobit users and earn cashback, except: Vietnam (card on waitlist, no cashback) and the United States (no sending to Oobit users). Brazil also has PIX, and Argentina, the Philippines and Vietnam have QR payments.
