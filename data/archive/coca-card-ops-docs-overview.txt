pay at 150M+ merchants worldwide and get rewarded . Spend your money everywhere COCA gives you both a virtual and a physical card , designed for everyday spending—coffee, groceries, Uber, online 
shopping, rent-related payments, and subscriptions. Add your COCA card to Apple Pay or Google Pay and pay anywhere cards are accepted. Easy ways to top up your Banking Account Adding money to COCA is 
simple. Choose the option that feels most familiar to you. Bank transfer (IBAN / SEPA) Get your own IBAN and send money to COCA just like you would with a regular bank account. Transfer EUR (SEPA) or 
USD, and your money is ready to spend. Card top up Add money instantly using your debit or credit card from another bank. It's fast, easy, and works the way you expect. Stablecoins You can also top 
up COCA with supported stablecoins on multiple blockchains directly to the Banking Account you control. Global Access You can order the card with a passport from almost any country, including those 
in the EEA, CIS, US, UK, Latin America, Asia-Pacific, MENA regions, and more. Card availability depends on your residence in one of the 54 supported countries, which include: EEA countries: Austria,
