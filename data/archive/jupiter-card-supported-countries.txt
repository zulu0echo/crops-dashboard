The Jupiter Card is issued by two partners, **Rain** and **DCS**, depending on the user's country of residence. Each user is assigned one card issuer automatically: **DCS** issues cards to residents of Australia, Japan, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore, South Korea, Taiwan, Thailand, and Vietnam; residents of the other supported countries get a **Rain**-issued card. It is not possible to hold cards from both issuers.

## Supported Countries

Eligibility is based on your country of residence, not your citizenship: a citizen of an unsupported country who resides in a supported one can be onboarded after completing identity verification. The list below shows where the Jupiter Card is actively offered.

<Note>
**Country not listed?** Eligibility is reviewed case by case during KYC, and proof of address is usually required.
</Note>
