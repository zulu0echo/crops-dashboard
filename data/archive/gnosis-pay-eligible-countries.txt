Eligible Countries for Gnosis Pay
Last updated 28 days ago
The Gnosis Pay Card is currently available to legal residents of selected countries in Europe and Latin America.
 To be eligible for a Gnosis Pay Card, 
you must be a legal resident
of one of the supported countries or regions listed below. We are actively expanding to new locations.

Restricted Countries for Card Usage
In line with international sanctions and security policies, Gnosis Card usage is prohibited in the following countries and regions. The card will not function if you are attempting a transaction from these locations.
Afghanistan
Belarus
Central African Republic
Crimea (region of Ukraine)
Cuba
