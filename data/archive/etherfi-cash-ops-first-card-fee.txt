Why a $10 fee may apply
Core members normally receive one free virtual Cash card. In some regions, regional pricing means the first virtual Cash card has a $10 fee instead.
Regions where the $10 first-virtual-card fee applies
Argentina, Angola, Bangladesh, Bolivia, Colombia, Ecuador, Indonesia, Malaysia, Mexico, Nigeria, Peru, Philippines, South Africa, and Thailand.
What to expect
The applicable fee is shown in the app before you order your card. This regional fee does not change whether ether.fi Cash is available in your region.
