How to invite friends and earn with your Fiat24 card
Invite friends to apply for a Bitget Wallet Card (Fiat24) and earn a bonus when they activate and spend. The more friends you refer, the more you earn.
Get your referral code or link
Go to "More" > "Referrals" > "Card" > "Invite now" to copy your unique referral code or invite link.
Share with friends
Share your code or link with friends who don't have a Bitget Wallet Card yet. If they don't apply through your link, they can manually enter your referral code within 7 days of card activation. | Note: Mainland China users are currently unable to apply for a new Fiat24 card.
Rewards
1. Card activation bonus is sent within 24 hours after your friend's card is approved. | 2. Spending commissions are distributed the next day to your Rewards account. | 3. Check and withdraw your rewards anytime from "Home" > "More" > "Rewards".
