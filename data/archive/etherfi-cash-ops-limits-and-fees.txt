Foreign Exchange (FX) Fees and Refunds
Where a transaction requires a currency conversion, ether.fi applies the prevailing FX base rate (~1%) and may add a foreign exchange margin on top of that base rate.
The added margin varies depending on the applicable FX base rate and on the user's tier, within the following ranges. The added FX fee is charged in addition to the underlying FX base rate and is reflected in the converted amount applied to the transaction.

For information on the VISA rate used, please visit https://usa.visa.com/support/consumer/travel-support/exchange-rate-calculator.html
Refund transactions are processed based on the FX rate applied at the time the refund is settled, not necessarily the same rate as the original purchase or the one shown on the Visa FX calculator for that date.
The Visa FX calculator provides indicative rates, but the final settlement rate can differ due to intermediary bank fees, network adjustments, or timing differences in how Visa processes the refund. These factors can lead to slight deviations, sometimes unfavorable, compared to the public reference rate.
