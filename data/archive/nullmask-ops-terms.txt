6.1 How these operations work. You sign each operation with your wallet. Our servers prepare the cryptographic proof, and our relayer submits the operation to Ethereum and pays the network gas. You pay the network and service fee described in section 7 in ETH from your private balance.

6.5 Swaps. Swaps inside the Pool are executed through a third-party decentralised exchange protocol, currently Uniswap V2. You choose the slippage tolerance. That protocol charges its own fee, and the price can move before your swap is executed.

7.1 Protocol fee. We charge a protocol fee on deposits and on withdrawals. Each fee is at most 1% of the amount. The current rates are shown in the application before you confirm.

7.3 Network and service fee. Transfers, swaps and withdrawals carry a network and service fee. It covers the network gas our relayer pays and our cost of running the Services. Our servers set its amount, and it may be higher than the actual network gas. The difference is not refunded.

7.4 Gas amount added to deposits. From the ETH you add to a deposit under section 5.1 we keep the gas for crediting and an amount towards the cost of screening. The rest is returned.

7.6 Changes. We may change fee rates, and how the network and service fee is calculated, at any time and without notice. The protocol fee stays within the 1% limit in section 7.1.
