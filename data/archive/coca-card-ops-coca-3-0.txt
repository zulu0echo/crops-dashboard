to COCA? Start Here. COCA is an app on your phone. It gives you a Visa card, a bank account number, and the ability to earn interest, cashback and discount perks — all without a traditional bank. 
Here&#x27;s what makes COCA different: with a normal bank, your money sits in an account the bank controls. They can freeze it, lend it out, or restrict it. With COCA, your money lives in a 
self-custodial account — a wallet that is mathematically yours. No one at COCA, no payment processor, no third party can access or move your funds unless you authorise the transaction yourself. Think 
of it like this: instead of handing your money to a bank to look after, COCA gives you the tools to look after it yourself — with the same easy experience you&#x27;d expect from any modern finance

balance, and up to 65% off hotels worldwide — all from one app. Why COCA 3.0 COCA started with a simple mission: let people spend, earn, and grow their money without relying on traditional banks — and 
without ever giving up control of their funds. Over time, we kept hearing the same question from our community: when can COCA be my main account? When can I receive my salary here? When can I use it 
the way I use a bank, but continue to have full ownership of my money? The honest answer was: not yet. You could receive and spend your stablecoins with the COCA card. You could earn cashback and 
APY. But you couldn&#x27;t receive or send bank transfers (fiat) directly into COCA. There was still a gap between COCA and everyday financial life. COCA 3.0 closes that gap. A bank account number. 
Real-time earnings. Full technical ownership of your funds. One app for everything. What&#x27;s New in COCA 3.0 Everything below is live now or arriving within weeks. 🔐 Full Self-Custody — Your Money, 
Technically Yours Before Most financial apps — including some that called themselves non-custodial — still had the ability to access, freeze, or restrict your funds behind the scenes. True ownership 
was a claim, not a technical reality. Now with COCA 3.0 With COCA 3.0, your money lives in a self-custodial account — a wallet secured by cryptography that only you control. COCA, payment processors, 
and any other intermediary cannot access, freeze, or move your funds. Every transaction requires your authorisation. What does self-custody actually mean? In traditional banking, your money 
isn&#x27;t really &#x27;yours&#x27; in the technical sense. It&#x27;s a number on the bank&#x27;s ledger. They control it. With self-custody, your funds are held in a cryptographic wallet — think of 
it like a digital safe that only opens with your key. Nobody else has a copy. With COCA 3.0, when you make a payment, only the exact amount of that transaction is authorised. The rest of your balance 
stays untouched in your wallet. It&#x27;s the digital equivalent of handing over cash at the till — precise, controlled, and nobody reaches into your pocket for more. How do I access my account 
without a seed phrase? Most crypto wallets make you write down a 12 or 24-word recovery phrase (a &#x27;seed phrase&#x27;). Lose it, and you lose your money. COCA uses a system called Privy — a 
security-first key management platform — that lets you log in with your email, no seed phrase needed. You still own your account and all the funds in it. Privy just makes accessing it feel simple and 
familiar. Privy is SOC 2 Type II certified (the same security standard used by enterprise software companies) and has been independently audited by multiple security firms. The result: full ownership 
of your money, with none of the complexity that usually comes with it. 🏦 Bank Account & Personal IBAN — Receive Your Salary in COCA Before To receive a salary, a bank transfer, or a payment from 
someone, you needed a separate traditional bank account. COCA and your everyday banking lived in two different places. Now with COCA 3.0 EUR bank accounts are now live. You get a personal IBAN — a 
unique account number, the same kind any bank would give you — so you can receive your salary, pay bills, and send or receive money, all from inside COCA. USD accounts with ACH support are coming 
soon. What is an IBAN? An IBAN (International Bank Account Number) is the standard account number for bank traders used across the European continents and a few neighboring territories. When someone 
wants to pay you — your employer, a client, a friend — they send it to your IBAN. It&#x27;s the same system every bank uses. Your COCA IBAN works exactly the same way. Give it to your employer, and 
your salary lands in COCA. Give it to a client, and their payment arrives in COCA. From there, the money is immediately available to spend with your COCA Card, or it starts earning APY the moment it 
arrives. You can also send money directly from COCA to other bank accounts — whether you&#x27;re paying bills or transferring funds to family and friends. And whenever you need cash, you can access it 
instantly with global ATM withdrawals. *EUR bank accounts are only available to residents of these countries. (see clause 1.3). ⚡ Instant APY — Your Balance Earns Every Second Before APY (interest on 
your balance) was paid out monthly. There was a minimum balance requirement to qualify, and if you moved your money before the payout date, you lost that month&#x27;s earnings entirely. Now with COCA 
3.0 APY now accrues in real time — every second of every day, on your spendable balance. No minimum balance. No lock-up. No waiting until the end of the month. The moment money lands in your account, 
it starts earning. What is APY? APY stands for Annual Percentage Yield — it&#x27;s the interest rate you earn on money sitting in your account. A 5% APY means that if you held $5,000 for a full year, 
you&#x27;d earn $250 in interest or more, through the power of compounding. With COCA, that interest now accrues continuously, not in monthly batches. Your balance stays fully spendable the entire 
time. There&#x27;s no lock-up period, no penalty for spending. Use your card, transfer money, or just let it grow — the choice is yours at every moment. Where does the yield come from? COCA generates 
yield by putting your balance to work through Morpho — a well-established, audited lending protocol. Your funds always remain under your control and are not handed over to COCA or any third party. 
They are not used for off-platform lending or borrowing. Within the protocol, borrowers provide collateral and pay interest to access liquidity, and that interest flows back to you. Risk is actively 
managed by Gauntlet , a specialist DeFi risk firm, which sets limits on how funds are allocated to keep returns sustainable and exposure controlled. This isn&#x27;t COCA paying interest from its own 
pocket. The yield comes from real financial activity, which is what makes it sustainable over time. How much you earn depends on your membership tier. Every tier earns 5% APY — what changes is the 
balance range in each tier. For example, the Starter tier earns 5% on the first $5,000, while higher tiers like Premium+ and Elite extend that range up to unlimited balances. These tier ranges are 
part of the COCA Loyalty program , designed to reward higher participation and give users a clear path to unlock more value as they move up tiers. 🎨 App Experience — Cleaner, Faster and More Intuitive
