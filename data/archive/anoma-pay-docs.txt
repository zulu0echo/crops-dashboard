https://docs.anoma.net/anoma-pay-trust-model (Last updated on April 2, 2026)
Proving Service Operator: Sees (temporarily) the unencrypted witness data. This is required to generate the zero-knowledge proofs and relay the transaction on your behalf. Witness data includes information about sender, receiver, asset, and amount. It does not, at any point in time, include authority to spend funds on any users' behalf.
Vast.ai Datacenters: Sees (temporarily) the unencrypted witness data. Vast.ai datacenters provide ISO certifications.
Compliance: AnomaPay is fully compliant with applicable laws and regulations (such as KYC and AML rules). The proving service operator implements consistent checks in order to ensure full compliance.

Even if the off-chain services go down, the funds on-chain are governed by the smart contract, which guarantees funds can only be moved with a valid proof. Any user can decide to run the off-chain infrastructure themselves and execute AnomaPay transactions powered by fully client-side proofs.
