Written by Mate October 28, 2024
 Every transaction you make with your card in a currency that is not USD has a 1.5% FX fee (foreign transaction fee) included in the currency rate. 
We may charge an origination fee whenever you make a loan. This fee will be included in the interest you pay on the installments associated with that loan.
