💡 Important notes
Supported payment methods may vary depending on your region and verification status.
Some payment methods are only available for specific currencies.
Processing times, fees, and transfer limits vary depending on the payment method.
Any applicable fees will be shown before you confirm your transaction.
Still have questions?
If you have any questions about supported payment methods or need help choosing the right deposit or withdrawal option, contact our support team via live chat or email at support@ether.fi.
