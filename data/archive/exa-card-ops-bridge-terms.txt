2. Description of the Service
The Service comprises two components: (i) a deposit service (the "Onramp Service") and (ii) a withdrawal service (the "Offramp Service"), both designed by Exa and provided through Bridge.
2.1 Onramp Service
The Onramp Service enables users to receive money deposits in US dollars (USD) or euros (EUR) via bank transfers (including ACH, SEPA, and wire transfers) originating from third-party bank accounts or financial platforms.
Funds received through the Onramp Service are automatically converted into USDC (USD Coin, a stablecoin backed 1:1 in US dollars) and credited to the user's wallet within the Exa App (the "Exa Account").
To access the Onramp Service, Exa may assign the user a virtual USD bank account in the United States (the "Virtual USD Account"), provided by Bridge, which will receive the transferred funds. This account is for the exclusive use of the user for the purposes of this Service.
2.2 Offramp Service
The Offramp Service enables users to send funds from their Exa Account to bank accounts, by converting USDC held in the Exa Account into US dollars (USD) or euros (EUR) and transferring the resulting fiat funds via bank transfer (including ACH, SEPA, and wire transfers) to a destination bank account designated by the user (the "Destination Account").
To use the Offramp Service, the user must provide accurate and complete details of the Destination Account. The user is solely responsible for the accuracy of such details. The Destination Account must be held in the user's own name, or the user must have proper legal authorization to send funds to it, in accordance with applicable law and Bridge's requirements. Exa and/or Bridge may restrict the Offramp Service to Destination Accounts held solely in the user's name in certain jurisdictions.
2.3 Conversion Criteria
Fund conversion is carried out according to the following criteria:
USD transfers (Onramp and Offramp): conversion between USD and USDC is performed at a 1:1 ratio.
EUR transfers (Onramp and Offramp): conversion between EUR and USDC is performed at the exchange rate in effect at the time Bridge processes the transaction. The approximate applicable exchange rate will be communicated to the user prior to confirming each transaction.
Exa acts as a facilitator of the Service. The processing, conversion, custody of funds in transit, and the Virtual USD Account infrastructure are the responsibility of Bridge. Exa does not issue or manage banking payment instruments in connection with this Service.

Up to USD 3,000 in cumulative onramped volume, OR
Up to 60 onramp transactions, whichever limit is reached first.

4.3 Post-Limit Pricing (Onramp)
Once a user has exceeded the sponsored limits described in section 4.2, the following fees apply per onramp transaction, based on currency:
USD (ACH): 0.4% of the transaction amount.
Wire (domestic US only): 1% of the transaction amount.
EUR: 0.7% of the transaction amount.
GBP: 1% of the transaction amount.
MXN: 1% of the transaction amount.
Post-limit fees will be automatically deducted from the received amount, prior to conversion to USDC and crediting to the user's Exa Account.
4.4 Offramp Fees

USD (ACH): 0.4% of the transaction amount.
Wire (domestic US only): 1% of the transaction amount.
EUR: 0.7% of the transaction amount.
Offramp fees will be automatically deducted from the amount to be sent, after conversion from USDC and prior to the transfer to the Destination Account. The net amount to be received will be communicated to the user prior to confirming each transaction.

5. Settlement Process
5.1 Onramp
Once a transfer is received in the user's Virtual USD Account, Bridge processes the conversion of funds to USDC and credits them to the Exa Account. Settlement times will depend on the type of transfer and banking processing times:
ACH transfers: generally between 1 and 3 business days.
Wire transfers (domestic US only): generally the same business day or the next.
SEPA transfers: generally between 1 and 2 business days.
5.2 Offramp
Once the user confirms an offramp transaction in the App, the corresponding USDC amount will be debited from the user's Exa Account, converted to the selected fiat currency, and the bank transfer to the Destination Account will be initiated by Bridge. Estimated settlement times are:
ACH transfers: generally between 1 and 3 business days.
Wire transfers (domestic US only): generally the same business day or the next.
SEPA transfers: generally between 1 and 2 business days.
Once an offramp transaction has been confirmed by the user and processing has begun, it cannot be modified or cancelled. Transfers sent to a Destination Account whose details were incorrectly provided by the user may be unrecoverable, and neither Exa nor Bridge shall be liable for losses arising from inaccurate or incomplete Destination Account information provided by the user.
5.3 General Provisions on Settlement
The timeframes indicated are estimates. Exa and Bridge do not guarantee specific processing times, as they may be affected by external factors such as banking hours, compliance verifications, or other circumstances beyond their control.
In the event that a transfer is rejected, returned, or cancelled for technical or compliance reasons, or at the instruction of the intervening financial institutions, Exa will manage the return of funds as follows: in the case of onramp transactions, to the user's originating account; in the case of offramp transactions, funds returned by the destination bank will be re-converted to USDC and credited back to the user's Exa Account, at the exchange rate in effect at the time of re-conversion where applicable.
